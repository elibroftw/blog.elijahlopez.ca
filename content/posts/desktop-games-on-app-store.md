This is a curated list based on games I think are worth while and also a list of simple games that could run on iPhone hardware but aren't available to install.

## Games on the App Store

- Stardew Valley
- AC: Mirage
- The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth
- Truck Simulator
- Terraria

## macOS Games

- Minecraft Java edition
- Sims 4
- Cyberpunk 2077

You can also use CrossOver to play other games but it has a subscription.

## Games not on Apple

- Meccha Chameleon (Windows-only)
- Lethal Company (Windows)
- Five Nights at Freddy's (Windows)
